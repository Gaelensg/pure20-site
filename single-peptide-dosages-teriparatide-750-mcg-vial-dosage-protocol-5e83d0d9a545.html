Research Period Amount per Administration U-100 Units (mL)
37 daily administrations (one-vial arithmetic) 20 mcg 8 units (0.08 mL)

Frequency: Once daily in the cited human trials and approved labeling.[1][2][3][4]

Research reference only: The 20 mcg amount is supported by human trials. The 37-administration period is only the number of complete 20 mcg calculations in one 750 mcg vial; it is not a trial duration, stability period, or recommendation.

Reconstitution Steps

  1. Clean the vial stopper and diluent stopper with alcohol; allow both to dry.
  2. Draw exactly 3.0 mL of bacteriostatic water with a new sterile syringe, then inject it slowly down the inside wall of the vial.
  3. Gently swirl or roll until dissolved. Do not shake.
  4. Label the vial with the compound, concentration, and reconstitution date. Refrigerate at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F) and protect from light.

Storage note: No compound-specific post-reconstitution stability or discard period has been established for this worked research-vial preparation. Follow the lot-specific product documentation for any shorter limit.

This page is for research calculation and educational reference only. It is not medical advice or a direction for human use.

04

Teriparatide Vial and Research Context

  • Reconstitute: Add 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water for a worked concentration of 250 mcg/mL.
  • Vial contents: 750 mcg Teriparatide.
  • U-100 conversion: 1 unit = 0.01 mL = 2.5 mcg.
  • Storage: Refrigerate at 2–8 °C and protect from light; approved-pen handling instructions are formulation-specific.

The 750 mcg vial math is not interchangeable with the approved teriparatide pen. The page preserves the published human amount while clearly separating it from research-vial preparation and stability assumptions.[1]

05

Teriparatide Dosage Evidence

Teriparatide is a parathyroid hormone analog studied extensively in osteoporosis. Randomized trials evaluated 20 mcg subcutaneously once daily in postmenopausal women, men, and glucocorticoid-induced osteoporosis populations.[2][3][4] Other research compared daily, weekly, and cyclic schedules rather than treating them as interchangeable.[5][6]

The approved product is a liquid multidose pen at 250 mcg/mL.[1] A 750 mcg lyophilized research vial has different formulation, sterility, excipient, stability, and delivery questions. The table on this page therefore uses the published 20 mcg amount only as a transparent arithmetic reference.

06

750 mcg Vial Calculation Notes

  • 750 mcg divided by 3.0 mL equals 250 mcg/mL.
  • One U-100 unit equals 0.01 mL or 2.5 mcg.
  • 20 mcg equals 8 units (0.08 mL).
  • One vial contains 37 complete 20 mcg calculated amounts with 10 mcg remaining. This is vial arithmetic, not a use-by period.
07

Human Research and Treatment Sequences

Clinical research has examined teriparatide alone, in combination with denosumab, and in treatment sequences.[7][8][9] Biosimilar and active-comparator trials also used the 20 mcg daily schedule.[10][11] A transdermal study used injected 20 mcg teriparatide as a comparator, illustrating that delivery systems must be evaluated separately.[12]

08

Safety and Formulation Limits

The official label contains formulation-specific warnings, adverse reactions, handling, and pen instructions.[1] Major osteoporosis guidelines discuss patient selection and monitoring for approved therapy.[13][14] Comparative osteoporosis research should not be used to infer that a third-party vial is clinically equivalent.[15]

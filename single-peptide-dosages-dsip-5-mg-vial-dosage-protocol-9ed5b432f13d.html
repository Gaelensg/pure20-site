DSIP Dosage Chart

Dosing & Reconstitution Guide

Educational guide for reconstitution and daily dosing

Standard / Gradual Approach (3 mL = ~1.67 mg/mL)

Week Daily Dose (mcg) Units (per injection) (mL)
Week 1 100 mcg 6 units (0.06 mL)
Week 2 150 mcg 9 units (0.09 mL)
Week 3 200 mcg 12 units (0.12 mL)
Weeks 4–8 250–300 mcg 15–18 units (0.15–0.18 mL)

Frequency: Inject once daily subcutaneously, typically in the evening before bedtime[5][6]. For ≤10-unit (≤0.10 mL) administrations (Weeks 1–2), consider 30- or 50-unit insulin syringes for improved readability.

Advanced / Extended Approach

Week Daily Dose (mcg) Units (per injection) (mL)
Week 5 350–400 mcg 21–24 units (0.21–0.24 mL)
Weeks 6–8+ 400–500 mcg 24–30 units (0.24–0.30 mL)

Note: Advanced dosing (beyond 300 mcg) is based on anecdotal experience; formal human studies have primarily used doses up to ~300 mcg daily[7]. Use the minimum effective dose and increase only if needed.

Reconstitution Steps

  1. Draw 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water with a sterile syringe.
  2. Inject slowly down the vial wall; avoid foaming.
  3. Gently swirl/roll until dissolved (do not shake).
  4. Label and refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F), protected from light.

Important: This guide is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice. For research use only. Not for human consumption.

DSIP Vial and Research Context

  • Reconstitute: Add 3.0 mL bacteriostatic water → ~1.67 mg/mL concentration.
  • Typical daily range: 100–300 mcg once daily (gradual titration); advanced up to 500 mcg.
  • Easy measuring: At 1.67 mg/mL, 1 unit = 0.01 mL ≈ 16.7 mcg on a U-100 insulin syringe.
  • Storage: Lyophilized: freeze at −20 °C (−4 °F); after reconstitution, refrigerate at 2–8 °C (35.6–46.4 °F); avoid freeze–thaw cycles.

DSIP—delta sleep-inducing peptide—is a naturally occurring nine-amino-acid research peptide studied in older human and preclinical work involving sleep architecture, sleep timing, and stress-related physiology[1][3][6]. The human literature is small, older, and often based on intravenous administration, so it does not validate the subcutaneous schedule displayed on this page[2][5]. This protocol keeps the vial calculations separate from those evidence limits.

DSIP 5 mg Dosage Protocol Overview

The 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 3.0 mL for an approximate concentration of 1.67 mg/mL. At that concentration, one U-100 syringe unit equals 0.01 mL, or approximately 16.7 mcg of DSIP. The dosage range, timing, and progression remain exactly as displayed in the chart above.

  • Vial strength: 5 mg
  • Final volume: 3.0 mL
  • Concentration: approximately 1.67 mg/mL
  • U-100 conversion: 1 unit = 0.01 mL ≈ 16.7 mcg

07

What Is the DSIP Peptide? Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide Explained

DSIP stands for delta sleep-inducing peptide, a naturally occurring nonapeptide first characterized in early experimental work on sleep-related neurophysiology[1]. DSIP is a neuropeptide used in research, not an established sleep medication. Reviews discuss possible roles in sleep regulation, endocrine signaling, and responses to stress while emphasizing the limits of the evidence[6][10].

08

Effects of Delta-Sleep-Inducing Peptide on Natural Sleep and Sleep Architecture

Research on DSIP has examined effects on sleep, including sleep onset, sleep timing, sleep efficiency, REM sleep, deep sleep, slow-wave sleep, delta sleep, and subjective sleep quality[3][5]. The findings were not uniform. These terms describe outcomes measured or discussed in early studies; they do not establish benefits of DSIP, natural sleep enhancement, improved sleep quality, or restorative sleep.

Preclinical experiments also reported sleep-related effects at low doses, but animal results cannot establish a human DSIP dosage protocol[7]. The current evidence does not show that DSIP reliably regulates the sleep cycle in a general population.

09

Human Findings and Evidence Limits

Published human research is limited to small studies from earlier decades, and some experiments used intravenous delivery rather than subcutaneous injection[2][3][5]. Those studies provide research context for DSIP and sleep architecture, but they do not validate the schedule or vial calculations on this page.

DSIP is not FDA-approved and is not approved by the FDA for a clinical use. No established clinical dosage range, standardized safety profile, or confirmed treatment role has been defined. It should not be presented as equivalent to approved sleep medications.

10

DSIP Research: Sleep, Rest and Recovery, and Stress

Preclinical studies have explored DSIP in models of stress protection and oxidative stress[4][11]. These findings support investigation of stress hormones, cellular defense, sleep and recovery, and rest and recovery pathways, but they are not proof of a therapeutic effect in humans or evidence that DSIP can regulate sleep.

11

Storage Instructions

Protein and peptide stability depends on formulation, temperature, moisture, light exposure, and handling[8][9]. Follow the storage directions shown in the four reconstitution checks above and the product’s lot-specific documentation. Keep the reconstituted vial refrigerated at 2–8 °C (36–46 °F), protect it from light, and avoid unnecessary agitation or repeated warming.

12

DSIP Dose, Safety Profile, and Interpretation

Because controlled modern human data are sparse, adverse-effect frequency, interaction risks, and long-term safety remain uncertain. DSIP may be discussed online as a sleep aid, but a favorable outcome in an early study or animal model is not evidence that DSIP is safe, well tolerated, or effective for insomnia.

This page is an educational calculation resource. It does not diagnose sleep disorders, replace professional medical evaluation, or recommend DSIP for human use.

13

Subcutaneous Measurement and Technique

U-100 syringe units are volume markings: 100 units equals 1 mL. They are not peptide international units. General subcutaneous-administration references describe site selection, skin preparation, and injection angle, but they do not validate the administration of DSIP or the schedule on this page[12][13].

DSIP 5 mg Dosage Chart FAQs

What concentration results from adding 3 mL to a 5 mg vial?

The final concentration is approximately 1.67 mg/mL.

How much DSIP is in one U-100 syringe unit?

At 1.67 mg/mL, one unit (0.01 mL) contains approximately 16.7 mcg.

Does the 5 mg vial strength change the research schedule?

Vial strength changes concentration, vial yield, and syringe-unit calculations. The schedule on this page remains the one displayed in the dosage chart.

What does the research say about DSIP for sleep?

Older small studies examined sleep timing and sleep architecture, but the evidence is inconsistent and insufficient to establish an approved treatment protocol.

Is DSIP FDA-approved?

No. DSIP is not an FDA-approved medication, and its clinical safety and effectiveness have not been established.

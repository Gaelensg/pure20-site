Private admin/dashboard/portal pages are intentionally left unchanged.

SAVING RULE
Visitors may build a stack and use Print/PDF without an account.
Persistent saving is now account-only.

If a logged-out visitor presses "Opslaan in account":
1. the current form is held temporarily in sessionStorage only for the login hand-off;
2. the visitor is sent to /account to register/sign in;
3. after returning, that temporary draft is restored;
4. the visitor can save it to their protected PURE20 account.

The old "Lokaal bewaren" button and persistent localStorage stack autosave are removed.

Existing cloud stacks and /account > Stacks continue to work.
No new database migration is required.

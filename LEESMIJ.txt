ADMIN
Business Dashboard > Customers:
- customer must have a registered PURE20 account
- customers without an account show "No account yet"
- customers with an account get a "Send plan" button

The send form supports:
- plan title
- goal/context
- multiple compounds
- amount / dose text
- frequency text
- timing text
- route text
- duration text
- compound note
- general note
- source/reference

PURE20 does not calculate, recommend or validate those values. The form only transmits the text entered by the admin.

Business Dashboard > Shared plans:
- see every sent plan
- see recipient
- see whether it has been viewed
- archive plans

CUSTOMER PROFILE
A new "Plans" tab appears in /account.
Unread plans show:
- a notification banner
- a numeric badge on the Plans tab
- a NEW label on the plan card

When the customer opens the plan:
- title, context and all compounds appear
- amount, frequency, timing, route and duration are displayed
- the plan becomes "viewed" in the admin dashboard
- general notes and source/reference are shown

SECURITY
- only a PURE20 admin can send/archive plans
- only the authenticated customer linked to that customer_id can read their plans
- customer cannot edit admin-sent plans
- plan data is protected by Supabase RLS

WHAT THE VISITOR SEES ON FIRST VISIT
- PURE20 welcome card
- language choice: Nederlands / English
- checkbox: 18 years or older
- checkbox: understands PURE20 products are offered for research purposes only and are not intended for use in humans or animals
- Continue button stays disabled until both boxes are checked

LANGUAGE
The selected language is saved in the existing 'pure20_language' setting.
After acceptance the page reloads once, so the entire website immediately starts in the selected language.

REMEMBERING ACCEPTANCE
Acceptance is saved in localStorage as:
pure20_access_gate_v1

The gate therefore does not appear again on every page after the visitor has accepted it on that device/browser.

TESTING AGAIN
To see the gate again, remove 'pure20_access_gate_v1' from the browser's site storage or use a fresh/private browser session.

SCOPE
The gate appears on customer-facing pages.
Admin, Wholesale Admin, Dashboard and Portal are excluded.

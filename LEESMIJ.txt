PURE20 CUSTOMER PORTAL + REFERRAL + ORDER TRACKING

Supabase is already configured for this update.

NEW:
- /account customer registration/login
- customer order dashboard
- status timeline: Placed > Accepted > Paid > Packed > Shipped > Delivered
- personalised referral link
- confirmed referral registration counter
- referred customers automatically receive 2% retail discount
- referral owner receives 3% credit when the referred order reaches Paid (or a later status)
- credit can be used on a next retail order
- cancellation reverses earned referral credit and refunds credit used on the cancelled order
- tracking number and carrier can be saved per order
- customer can track an order or paste any tracking code in the account tracking tab
- general tracking is powered by the official 17TRACK widget

ADMIN WORKFLOW:
Dashboard > Orders:
- choose order status
- enter tracking number
- optionally enter carrier
- optionally enter a customer-facing status message
- Save order

REFERRAL LOGIC:
- A referral link looks like /account?ref=XXXXXXXXXX
- The referral relationship is fixed at registration.
- The new customer gets 2% automatically while logged in.
- The owner earns 3% only after status becomes Paid/Packed/Shipped/Delivered.
  This prevents unpaid/fake orders from creating spendable credit.
- Credit use appears in the cart and is deducted server-side.

IMPORTANT:
The retail customer must be logged in while placing the order for referral discount and referral credit to apply.
